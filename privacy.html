Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current CrateSeek MVP. The app does not require an account, does not sell personal information and does not use advertising trackers.

1. Overview

CrateSeek is a music-discovery app for producers. Most preferences and library activity stay on your device. We process a small amount of pseudonymous information to operate the catalog, understand whether core features work and receive feature requests.

2. Information we process

Stored on your device: onboarding status, filter and playback preferences, saved tracks, listening history, IDs of tracks already shown, an anonymous installation ID, and a short queue of analytics events waiting to be sent. Native authentication credentials, if account features are introduced and used, are stored using the operating system’s secure credential storage.

Sent to CrateSeek: random anonymous installation and session IDs; limited event names such as app opens, onboarding completion, sample views, playback, skips, saves, filter use and session duration; relevant recording or playback-source IDs; timestamps; app version and platform; a broad technical error category when the app encounters a JavaScript crash; and the text you deliberately submit through Request a Feature. Crash events do not include the error message, stack trace, screen contents or user-entered text. We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, microphone recordings or music files in the current MVP.

Technical request data: hosting and infrastructure providers may receive standard network information such as IP address, user agent and request time. CrateSeek uses one-way hashed, short-lived request buckets for abuse prevention and does not store raw IP addresses in its application database.

3. How we use information

  • Provide playable catalog results and remember tracks already shown.
  • Maintain saved tracks, history and your preferences.
  • Measure whether onboarding, playback, discovery and saving work reliably.
  • Prevent spam, bots and excessive requests.
  • Review feature ideas you choose to submit.
  • Protect CrateSeek and comply with legal obligations.

For users in the European Economic Area, processing necessary to deliver requested features is based on performance of the service; limited reliability analytics and abuse prevention are based on our legitimate interests in operating and securing CrateSeek.

4. Providers and external playback

CrateSeek uses Supabase for its catalog database and server-side functions; PostHog Cloud for privacy-minimal product analytics when configured; GitHub Pages for this website; and YouTube’s permitted embedded player for playable previews. PostHog receives pseudonymous events and is configured without person profiles, session replay, broad interaction autocapture or GeoIP enrichment. CrateSeek does not host, extract or offer downloads of third-party music. When a YouTube player or external link is opened, Google/YouTube may receive device and network information under its own terms and privacy policy. Discogs and MusicBrainz provide catalog metadata; CrateSeek’s trusted catalog tools access them server-side or on the project owner’s machine.

Advertising and RevenueCat SDKs are not present in the current MVP. This policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be reviewed before either is enabled. We do not authorize current providers to use CrateSeek’s private feature-request inbox for their own advertising. Their independent processing is governed by their own policies.

5. Retention and security

Device data remains until you clear the app’s data or remove the app. Feature requests and pseudonymous analytics are retained only while reasonably useful for product planning, reliability, security and legal needs, after which they are deleted or aggregated. Security controls include encrypted transport, least-privilege database access, row-level security, input limits, rate limits and restricted server-side keys.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Please do not place confidential, legally privileged or highly sensitive information in a feature request.

6. Your choices and rights

You can remove local CrateSeek data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection concerning information associated with your installation ID. Because CrateSeek does not ask for identifying account details in the MVP, we may need that installation ID to locate a request and may be unable to connect fully anonymous records to you.

7. Children

CrateSeek is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so it can be reviewed and removed.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated, and material changes will be communicated through the app or website where appropriate.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method listed on the CrateSeek project owner’s GitHub profile. Do not include private information in a public GitHub issue.
